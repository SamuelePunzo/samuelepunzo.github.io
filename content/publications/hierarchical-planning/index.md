abstract: "Hi-LeWM adds a high-level planner over latent subgoals to a frozen LeWorldModel. Experiments on goal-conditioned control tasks show that temporal hierarchy alone does not guarantee better planning. A mismatch between searched macro-actions and the low-level controller can produce ineffective subgoals. Constraining the search around macro-actions encoded from training trajectories, together with appropriate execution timing, recovers improvements at longer horizons."
